About

Hi, we're Rob and Petronella.

Common Ground is run by Rob Lewis and Petronella. Petronella has worked in digital marketing for over 15 years. Rob brings a long history in the geoengineering and construction sector, including his work with GeoEnviro.

Between us we've spent a lot of time on construction sites and around engineering projects, so we understand how these businesses really work and the problems they face.

We both kept seeing the same thing. A lot of companies were paying for marketing that didn't work, often from big providers who don't understand the industry.

So that's what we do now. We work closely with each business to help them get seen, build trust and bring in the right kind of leads, with simple, honest marketing that makes sense.
